Current Research

Mechanistic interpretability · large language models

Master's thesis · Northeastern University · 2026-present

When a causal intervention works, did we actually find the model's mechanism?

Studying the robustness and faithfulness of causal subspaces discovered with Distributed Alignment Search (DAS).

I am testing whether a successful intervention reflects a reusable internal computation or only a task-specific way to force the desired output. The project follows what happens after the patch, rather than stopping once the counterfactual answer flips.

  • Built PyTorch and PyVene pipelines for activation interventions across layers, seeds, and lexical distributions.
  • Evaluate causal effectiveness, isolation, downstream persistence, and out-of-distribution behavior together.
  • Compare natural counterfactual trajectories with patched trajectories to find where apparently successful interventions begin to diverge.
What I am trying to separate

A patch that changes the answer from a patch that reproduces the model's natural causal pathway.
